Where It Goes

What Your Support Does

We keep overheads tiny. Donations fund the work, not the admin.

🎫

Free Training Places

Subsidised and free places for families who couldn't otherwise afford professional training help.

🏠

Rescue Dog Rehab

Specialist behaviour support so newly adopted dogs settle into their homes — and stay there.

📣

Community Outreach

Free workshops in schools and community centres on dog safety, body language and responsible ownership.
